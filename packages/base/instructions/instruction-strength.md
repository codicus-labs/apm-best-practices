---
description: What the strengths Must and Prefer at the start of an instruction mean
load: always
---

Every instruction of these packages starts with its strength:

- **Must.** Departing from the instruction needs the owner's consent.
- **Prefer.** The instruction is the default; departing from it needs a written, researched reason.

An instruction ending in "Enforced by …" names the config, package or check that already enforces it. Instructions name no versions: a tool an instruction names means its current release.
